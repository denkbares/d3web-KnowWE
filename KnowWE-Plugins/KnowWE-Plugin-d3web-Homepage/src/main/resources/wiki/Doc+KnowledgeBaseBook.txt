! KnowledgeBaseBook

! Definition
The KnowledgeBaseBook markup can be used to specify the order\\
in which the DiaFlux diagrams shall be printend into the KnowledgeBaseBook.\\


Use as follows:
{{{
%%KnowledgeBaseBook 
  @DiaFluxOrder: DiaFluxA
  @DiaFluxOrder: DiaFluxC
  @DiaFluxOrder: DiaFluxD
%
}}}
