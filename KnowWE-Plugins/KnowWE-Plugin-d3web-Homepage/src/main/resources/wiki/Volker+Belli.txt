! About Me

My main focus is on conception and project management of knowledge-based solutions.

I'm the founder and CEO of the denkbares GmbH, working in the field of diagnostic systems since 1996. From 1999 till now I consulted companies in diagnostic projects, analyzing the requirements and the project goals, elaborating cost-benfit-analysis, assessing technical feasibility and deriving a diagnostic concept for the project. In my over 15 year within that area, I attended more than 50 projects, mostly as project manager and analyst. For special challenges, I like to rely on my grown network of product suppliers, researchers and diagnostic experts.
